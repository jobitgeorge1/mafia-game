Roles

  • 🔫 Mafia Eliminate one villager each night. Win when Mafia ≥ remaining villagers.
  • 🔍 Detective Investigate one player each night to reveal their alignment.
  • 💉 Doctor Protect one player per night. Can only self-save once.
  • 🏘️ Villager Use discussion and logic to vote out the Mafia during the day.

Phases

  1. Night — Mafia chooses a target. Doctor saves. Detective investigates. (30s)
  2. Day Reveal — The night's outcome is announced. (5s)
  3. Discussion — All alive players debate who the Mafia is. (90s)
  4. Vote — Vote to eliminate a suspect. Majority required. (40s)

Player Count

  • 4–5 players → 1 Mafia
  • 6–8 players → 2 Mafia
  • 9–12 players → 3 Mafia

Roles

  • 👤 Civilian You have the real word. Give hints without making it obvious.
  • 🕵️ Undercover You have a similar but different word. Blend in — don't get caught!
  • ❓ Blank You have no word. Listen carefully and bluff your way through.

Phases

  1. Word Reveal — Each player privately sees their word (or nothing if Blank).
  2. Clue Giving — Players take turns giving exactly one one-word clue.
  3. Discussion — Open discussion to find the imposters.
  4. Vote — Vote to eliminate the most suspicious player.
  5. Blank Guess — If the Blank (or last Undercover) is eliminated, they get one chance to guess the civilian word and steal the win.

Win Conditions

  • 🏆 Civilians win when all Undercovers and Blanks are eliminated.
  • 🕵️ Undercover wins when they outnumber civilians, or correctly guess the civilian word when eliminated last.
  • ❓ Blank wins by correctly guessing the civilian word when eliminated, or being the last one standing.
